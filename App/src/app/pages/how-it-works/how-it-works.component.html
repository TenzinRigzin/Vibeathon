How the agent adapts over time

This is not a chatbot. It’s a career co-pilot that reasons, plans, and outputs actions.

Agent workflow

  1. Skill extraction & mapping — We extract structured skills from your GitHub, LinkedIn, or resume and map them to a common competency model.
  2. Market alignment — Your chosen dream role’s requirements are compared to your profile so we identify missing competencies (technical and soft).
  3. Agentic planning — The system plans a 30-day sequence of tasks, checkpoints, and resources. As you mark progress, the plan can be updated (e.g. focus more on weak areas).
  4. Personalization — The roadmap reflects your current level, time constraints, and career goal so it stays relevant and achievable.

What you get

  • A working agent prototype with multi-step reasoning (profile → role → gaps → roadmap).
  • A personalized 30-day learning tree with themes, daily goals, resources, and checkpoints.
  • A clear explanation of how the agent adapts: when you complete days or change your profile, gaps and recommendations can be recomputed so the roadmap evolves with you.
